# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be useful, rigorous, and welcoming. Please be respectful, precise, and constructive in issues, reviews, and pull requests.

## Expected behavior

- criticize ideas, not people
- provide evidence when reporting bugs or risks
- avoid hostility, harassment, and dismissive language

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior makes collaboration unsafe or unproductive.

Report serious concerns to `opensource@hesia.eu`.
